# Data license and attribution

The source measurement data in this repository comes from the Open Observatory of Network Interference (OONI).

OONI data is published under the terms in the [OONI data license](https://github.com/ooni/license/tree/master/data). Use and redistribution of OONI-derived records in this repository remain subject to those terms. The source field, measurement UID, official measurement URL, and preserved source summary provide attribution and traceability.

Researchers should also review the [OONI data policy](https://ooni.org/about/data-policy/) before processing or republishing measurements.
